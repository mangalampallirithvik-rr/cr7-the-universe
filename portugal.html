04 / National team

PORTUGAL

A career with Portugal measured in caps, goals, responsibility and landmark tournament nights.

National pride

For the badge.

Ronaldo made his senior Portugal debut in 2003 and became a central figure across multiple generations. His international career includes the emotional Euro 2016 title and the 2019 UEFA Nations League victory on home soil.

Leadership has taken different forms over time: direct goal threat, experience in tournament preparation, standards in training and responsibility in high-pressure moments. Portugal’s achievements remain team accomplishments, built by coaches and teammates as well as its captain.

2016

European champions

Portugal beat France in the final after Ronaldo was forced off injured. The team finished the job in extra time.

2019

Nations League

Portugal won the inaugural UEFA Nations League finals on home soil.

Legacy

A record-setting career

His longevity and international scoring record have made him a reference point in men’s international football.

The meaning

Country and identity

For fans, the Portugal chapter connects an individual career to national memory and collective celebration.
