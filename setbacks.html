CR7 / Setbacks

THE FALLS & THE COMEBACKS

Defeats, injuries, criticism and difficult transitions are part of the record too. A balanced look at the moments that tested Ronaldo’s career.

CHAPTER 01

EURO 2004: a final lost at home

As a teenager, Ronaldo reached the EURO 2004 final with Portugal. The hosts lost 1–0 to Greece in Lisbon, a deeply disappointing result after a remarkable tournament run. It was an early reminder that individual talent cannot guarantee a team trophy.

CHAPTER 02

Injury at the 2014 World Cup

Ronaldo arrived at the 2014 World Cup with a knee problem, and Portugal were eliminated in the group stage. The campaign showed how injuries and team context can limit even a world-class player. The lesson is not that a career was over, but that physical readiness and team balance matter.

CHAPTER 03

EURO 2016 final: forced to watch

Ronaldo left the EURO 2016 final against France injured in the first half. Unable to continue playing, he watched from the touchline as Portugal went on to win after extra time. A painful individual moment became a collective triumph, illustrating that leadership can continue beyond the pitch.

CHAPTER 04

World Cup exits and public pressure

Portugal exited the 2018 World Cup in the round of 16 and the 2022 tournament in the quarter-finals. The later tournament also brought intense debate about selection, tactics and Ronaldo’s role. These outcomes are part of the record, but they do not erase earlier achievements or explain every team result on their own.

CHAPTER 05

Manchester United return: a complicated chapter

Ronaldo’s 2021 return to Manchester United included important goals but unfolded amid managerial changes and inconsistent team performances. His 2022 departure followed a public dispute and a difficult season. It remains a reminder that a famous name alone cannot resolve structural issues within a club.

CHAPTER 06

What the setbacks reveal

The strongest career lesson is not that failure is glamorous; it is that progress depends on responding to it. Ronaldo’s career repeatedly involved adaptation: a different position, new tactical demands, recovery from injury, changing teammates and new leagues. The setbacks belong in the story alongside the trophies.
