## Remote photographs

Photographs loaded from Wikimedia Commons or other external sources remain subject to the license and conditions on their individual source pages. `credits.html` links to several known source pages. For every image actually displayed on the published website:

1. Open the exact source file page.
2. Record the creator/photographer, source URL, and exact license version.
3. Add the attribution required by that license.
4. If the license requires share-alike for adaptations, comply with that condition.
5. Confirm that the URL still points to the intended file and that public use is permitted.

A photograph being available on Wikimedia Commons does not mean every use is unrestricted. Personality rights, trademarks, privacy, and local law may also apply.
