THE STORY BEHIND CR7

From Madeira to the biggest stages in world football: the milestones, sacrifices and reinventions behind a long career.

CHAPTER 01

Madeira: where it started

Cristiano Ronaldo dos Santos Aveiro was born in Funchal, on the Portuguese island of Madeira, on 5 February 1985. His early life was shaped by a close family, a love of football and a determination to improve. He joined local side Andorinha as a child, where his father worked, before moving through Nacional and into Sporting CP’s academy.

CHAPTER 02

The leap to Lisbon

At about 12, Ronaldo moved from Madeira to Lisbon to attend Sporting’s academy. Leaving home at that age was a major personal challenge, but it gave him access to elite coaching and competition. His pace, close control and confidence quickly set him apart.

CHAPTER 03

Manchester United: talent becomes a star

After impressing against Manchester United in a 2003 friendly, Ronaldo signed for the English club. Under Sir Alex Ferguson he developed from an exciting winger into a more decisive scorer, winning the Premier League and the 2008 Champions League, as well as his first Ballon d’Or.

CHAPTER 04

Real Madrid: record-setting years

Ronaldo moved to Real Madrid in 2009 and produced one of the most prolific spells in club football. His goals, athleticism and relentless standards helped Madrid win four Champions League titles during his time there. The club records 450 goals in 438 appearances across competitions.

CHAPTER 05

Italy, return to Manchester and a new challenge

At Juventus, Ronaldo won domestic honours and adapted to a different league. His return to Manchester United in 2021 brought memorable goals amid a difficult team period. In 2023, he began a new chapter with Al Nassr, extending his career into a different football landscape.

CHAPTER 06

Portugal: the long international journey

Ronaldo made his senior Portugal debut in 2003. The 2004 European Championship final defeat was painful, but he later captained Portugal to the EURO 2016 title and the 2019 UEFA Nations League. His international career has spanned multiple generations of teammates and several major tournaments.
